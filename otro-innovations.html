Trade OTRO

trade.otro.bd · by enquiry

Sourcing from Asia is often slowed by communication gaps, quality inconsistencies and logistical opacity. Trade OTRO is a B2B sourcing agency, not a dropshipping middleman. It acts as the English-speaking representative for importers and wholesalers, managing supplier vetting, factory audits, quality control, Incoterms (FOB, CIF, DDP) and logistics, and turning buyer expectations into clear mandates for Chinese manufacturers.

  • Supplier vetting
  • Factory audits & QC
  • Incoterms & logistics
  • China–global sourcing

Shop OTRO

Shop OTRO anchors the ecosystem in the Bangladeshi consumer market: a B2C platform with curated products tuned to local buying behaviour. Cash-on-delivery and last-mile partnerships with Pathao and RedX make it practical for emerging-market customers. It is both a revenue stream and a live testing ground for OTRO's marketing and logistics.

OTRO Tours

tours.otro.bd · by enquiry

OTRO Tours is a bridge service provider for business, medical and cultural travel. It removes language barriers and simplifies visas, fair registration, hospital bookings and on-ground logistics.

Business travel & trade fairs

Visa invitation support, Canton Fair and Yiwu access, hotels, airport pickup, daily fair transport, English–Mandarin interpretation, supplier meetings and factory visits.

Medical facilitation

Hospital matching by condition and budget, appointments, medical visa support, cost estimates, records translation, hospital interpretation and recovery logistics. This covers logistics and coordination only, never medical advice or treatment decisions.

Individual & group tours

Custom itineraries across Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an, Guilin and beyond; visa help, hotels, domestic flights, English-speaking guides, high-speed rail and WeChat Pay / Alipay setup. Prices are quoted per itinerary.

  • Trade fairs
  • Medical facilitation
  • Group & individual tours
  • Interpretation

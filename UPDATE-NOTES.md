# sheikhirfan.com redesign: what to merge

Copy these files over your existing site folder. They add `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/site.js` and three new fonts, and they leave your old `assets/css/styles.css` alone so `hau/` pages keep working.

Still needed from your folder (not in this package): `assets/images/irfan-portrait.jpg`, `assets/images/og-cover.png`, `assets/apple-touch-icon.png`, and the `hau/` folders.
Not yet restyled: `hau/academic`, `hau/eca`, `hau/mib`, `hau/mne` (they still use the old design).

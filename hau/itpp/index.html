Five moves: access into capability五步：把“接入”变成“能力”

  1. Make inputs at home — yarn, fabric, parts本地生产纱线、面料、零部件
  2. Host China-Plus-One factories — with skills and technology that stay承接产业转移，要求技术与技能留下
  3. More partners and buyers — China, Japan, EU, others伙伴多元，不被单一绑定
  4. Deliver faster — land, permits, clear pipeline土地、审批、项目落地更快
  5. Open the EV window — lithium cells and packs, stable rules电动车与锂电池：规则稳定、吸引投资

Privacy policy.

A plain-language account of what this website collects, why it collects it, and the choices you have.

Effective 24 October 2026 Applies to sheikhirfan.com Includes OTRO pages

This website is operated by Md Irfan Hossain, an individual based in Huai'an, Jiangsu, China. The OTRO INNOVATIONS LLC pages on this domain are operated in connection with that company. This policy explains how personal information is handled across both.

1. Information you give us

You can use most of this website without providing personal information. When you choose to get in touch, you may provide:

  • Your name and contact details, including email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and social-media profile.
  • The subject and content of your message, including any files or links you choose to share.
  • Information about a project, business, order, or application that is relevant to your enquiry.

The contact form on this site does not send your message to a server. It prepares an email in your own email application, which you then choose to send. The message reaches us only if you send it.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, the hosting provider may record limited technical information in server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, the pages requested, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to keep the site available, secure, and performing correctly.

This site does not currently use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics. Fonts are served from this domain rather than from a third-party font service. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the new tool is used.

3. How information is used

  • To reply to your enquiry and continue the conversation you started.
  • To prepare proposals, provide services, and manage an ongoing engagement.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.
  • To protect the rights of Md Irfan Hossain, OTRO INNOVATIONS LLC, and the people we work with.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your message to sign you up for marketing without your consent.

4. Sharing

Personal information is shared only where there is a practical need and an appropriate safeguard. This may include:

  • Service providers that help run the site, email, or business operations, such as hosting and email providers.
  • Suppliers, logistics partners, or professional advisers involved in a project you have asked us to support.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect a legal right.
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of the business, subject to equivalent protections.

5. International transfers

Work may take place across Bangladesh, China, the United States, and other markets. Your information may therefore be processed in a country other than the one you live in. Where that happens, reasonable steps are taken to ensure the information remains protected and is used only for the purposes described here.

6. Retention

Enquiry and project information is kept for as long as needed to respond, provide the relevant service, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised. You may ask for earlier deletion, subject to any records that must be retained by law.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information, including encrypted connections to this website and access controls on business accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information is held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Ask for information to be deleted or for its use to be restricted.
  • Object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to a relevant data-protection authority.

To make a request, email me@sheikhirfan.com. A response will normally be provided within 30 days. You may be asked to verify your identity before a request is actioned.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Personal information from children is not knowingly collected. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please get in touch so it can be removed.

10. Third-party links

This site links to services such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp, ResearchGate, and publication pages. Those services run under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they collect or use your information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, the services, or the applicable rules change. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised. Material changes will be communicated clearly where practical.

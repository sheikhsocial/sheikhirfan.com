03 · Case 1 案例一

Teesta River Project提斯塔河综合治理与修复项目

$750M
Phase 1 cost, of which Bangladesh seeks $550M from China; work planned 2026–2029.一期约7.5亿美元,孟方申请中方贷款5.5亿美元;计划2026–2029年实施。
India’s concernShared river, and the site lies near the Siliguri Corridor. India says it has conveyed its views to Dhaka.印度关切:跨境河流,且靠近西里古里走廊;印方已向达卡表明立场。
Update on my draftOnce stalled; in 2026 China pledged support and a feasibility study is done. Friction now shows as diplomacy, not a halt.此前搁置;2026年中方承诺支持,可行性研究已完成。

06 · Mechanisms 作用机制

How third-party policy shapes business第三方政策如何影响商业决策

Diplomatic pressurePublic statements and “debt-trap” narratives shape opinion and timing.外交施压与“债务陷阱”叙事。
Counter-offersIndia offered to invest in Teesta in 2024 and earlier planned zones at Mongla.替代方案:印度曾提出参与提斯塔并规划经济区。
Sanctions risk (potential)Banks and contractors may hesitate if secondary sanctions loom, as in the Huawei case.潜在风险:二级制裁使银行与承包商犹豫(类比华为)。
07 · Conclusion 结论

Hedging is the host-country strategy对冲平衡:东道国的应对策略

For governmentsTake Chinese capital while keeping Indian ties: strategic hedging.政府:引入中资,同时维系对印关系。
For firmsPrice in third-party political risk when bidding in South Asia.企业:投标时须计入第三方政治风险。
Huawei parallelNational policy is not neutral: it can enable or block international business.与华为案例一致:国家政策可促成,也可阻断国际商务。

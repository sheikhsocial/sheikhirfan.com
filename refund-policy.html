Refund policy.

A fair, plain-language framework for cancellations, refunds, and work that has already been delivered.

Effective 24 October 2026 OTRO INNOVATIONS LLC Service engagements

This policy applies to paid services provided through OTRO INNOVATIONS LLC or by Md Irfan Hossain. Where a signed proposal or service agreement sets different terms, that agreement takes priority.

Before you pay: every engagement should have a written scope, fee, timeline, and cancellation term. If anything is unclear, ask for it in writing before making a payment.

1. Cancellation before work begins

If you cancel in writing before work on the engagement has started, you are eligible for a refund of amounts paid, less:

  • Payment-processor or bank fees that have already been incurred.
  • Third-party costs already committed on your behalf, such as university application fees, supplier samples, or paid research.
  • Any expressly non-refundable deposit stated in the applicable proposal.

2. Cancellation after work begins

Once work has started, fees are earned for the work completed and for time reserved for the engagement. A refund will be calculated by:

  1. Taking the total fee for the agreed scope.
  2. Subtracting the value of completed milestones, work delivered, and non-recoverable third-party costs.
  3. Refunding the remaining balance, if any.

Where work is billed hourly, time already spent is payable. Where work is billed by milestone, completed milestones are payable even if later milestones are cancelled.

3. Completed work and digital deliverables

Fees for completed work, delivered files, published campaigns, reports, or advisory sessions are non-refundable. If a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, please raise it promptly so it can be corrected. Corrections within the agreed scope are handled at no additional cost.

4. Third-party and external costs

University application fees, visa fees, supplier deposits, freight charges, and similar third-party costs are governed by the provider's own rules. OTRO can request a cancellation or refund on your behalf, but cannot guarantee one. Any amount returned by a third party will be passed on, less any unrecoverable handling cost.

5. Study-abroad and application services

Admission, scholarship, and visa outcomes are decided by universities and authorities, not by OTRO. Fees are for the guidance and preparation work provided, not for a guaranteed outcome. Application fees paid to institutions are separate and are generally non-refundable.

6. Sourcing and import–export engagements

Supplier deposits, sample costs, inspection fees, freight, duties, and financing charges are committed to third parties and follow their terms. If a supplier fails to perform, OTRO will make reasonable efforts to recover funds or identify an alternative, but is not responsible for a third party's independent acts or omissions. Any recovery received will be handled transparently.

7. How to request a refund

Email otroinnovationsllc@gmail.com with:

  • Your name and the email used for the engagement.
  • The service, invoice number, and payment date.
  • The reason for the request and the outcome you are seeking.

Requests are acknowledged within 7 business days. A decision is normally provided within 14 business days, after reviewing the scope and work completed. Approved refunds are returned by the original payment method within 14 business days, subject to the payment provider's processing time.

8. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first. Raising a chargeback without allowing a reasonable opportunity to resolve the issue may delay a resolution. We will cooperate with a payment provider's review and provide records of the agreed scope and work performed.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated for future engagements. The version in effect at the time of payment, or the terms in a signed agreement, govern that engagement. The effective date above shows when this version was published.

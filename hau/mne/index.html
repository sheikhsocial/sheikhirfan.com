Host-country regulation · 东道国监管

China’s FDI legal framework中国外商投资法律框架

Model 1 · 模式一

No special FDI law

General commercial law applies.

无专门外资法,适用一般商法。

Model 2 · 模式二

No unified code

Rules scattered across sector statutes.

无统一法典,规则分散于各行业法规。

Model 3 · China · 模式三(中国)

Unified FDI code

A dedicated Foreign Investment Law.

统一外资法典:专门的《外商投资法》。

Foreign Investment Law · 外商投资法

Adopted in 2019 and in force since 1 January 2020. It replaced the separate laws on equity joint ventures, cooperative joint ventures and wholly foreign-owned firms.

2019年通过,2020年1月1日起施行,取代了中外合资、中外合作和外资企业三部单行法。

Negative list system · 负面清单制度

Sectors off the list get national treatment at entry. Listed sectors carry equity caps or bans, and car making was one until its caps were phased out.

清单外领域享有准入前国民待遇;清单内领域有股比限制或禁入。汽车制造曾在清单内,股比限制已逐步取消。
Sources: Norton Rose Fulbright; China Daily · 资料来源
The policy under analysis · 分析的政策

Lifting the auto equity cap取消汽车行业外资股比限制

1994

Foreign carmakers capped at a 50% stake in any local venture.

外国车企在任何合资企业中持股不得超过50%。
2018

On 17 April the state planner announced a staged removal. The cap ended for electric and plug-in hybrid makers.

4月17日国家发改委宣布分阶段取消;电动车和插电混动车企率先放开。
2020

The Chinese-ownership requirement lifted for commercial vehicles.

商用车的中方持股要求取消。
2022

Lifted for passenger cars. The limit of two joint ventures per foreign investor also ended.

乘用车放开;每个外国投资者最多设两家合资企业的限制同时取消。

Why it matters · 重要意义

Electric vehicles went first, so Tesla could build a wholly owned plant four years before the cap fell for passenger cars.

电动车率先放开,特斯拉因此比乘用车企业早四年建成独资工厂。
Sources: Reuters via WardsAuto; Norton Rose Fulbright · 资料来源
The case: Tesla Shanghai · 案例:特斯拉上海

How the policy toolkit was applied to Tesla政策工具如何用于特斯拉

Sectors & equity · 行业与股比

100% ownership. The cap was waived for EV makers, so Tesla Shanghai is not a joint venture.

100%控股。电动车企业不再受股比限制,特斯拉上海不是合资企业。

Land & resources · 土地与资源

864,885 m² in Lingang for RMB 973 million, contract signed 17 October 2018.

临港土地864,885平方米,9.73亿元人民币,2018年10月17日签约。

Taxes & incentives · 税收与优惠

15% income tax reported through 2023 versus the 25% standard, plus a purchase-tax exemption for EV buyers.

据报道2023年前适用15%所得税(标准税率25%),另有购车免购置税。

Screening & approval · 审批

Still licensed. Vehicle manufacturing needs a ministry licence even after the cap went.

仍需许可。取消股比限制后,整车制造仍须获得主管部门许可。

Employment rules · 用工要求

Local hiring pledge. Reported commitment of one local hire per US$1.8 million of revenue.

本地雇佣承诺。据报道每180万美元收入雇用一名本地员工。

State-bank finance · 银行融资

Up to RMB 11.25 billion (about US$1.6 billion) from four Chinese banks, December 2019.

四家中资银行提供最高112.5亿元人民币(约16亿美元)贷款,2019年12月。

Analysis · 分析

Policy evaluation政策评价

Why it worked · 为何成功

  • Predictable. Dated, staged removal of the cap gave investors certainty.可预期:取消股比限制有时间表、分阶段,给投资者确定性。
  • Packaged. Equity, land, tax and credit arrived together, cutting time to market.一揽子:股比、土地、税收和信贷同时到位,缩短了上市时间。
  • Mutual. China gained jobs, local suppliers, exports and a strong rival for domestic EV makers.互利:中国获得就业、本地供应商、出口,以及国内电动车企的强劲对手。

Limits and risks · 局限与风险

  • Time-limited perks. The 15% tax rate ran through 2023.优惠有期限:15%税率执行至2023年。
  • Leased land. Tesla owns the factory but not the land.土地为租用:特斯拉拥有工厂,但不拥有土地。
  • Dependence. Licences, capex and hiring pledges bind Tesla, and US–China relations can shift the outlook.依赖性:许可、投资与雇佣承诺约束特斯拉,中美关系也可能改变前景。

Takeaways · 结论

Conclusion总结

1
Policy shapes entry mode

Removing the equity cap let Tesla choose full ownership over a joint venture.

政策塑造进入模式
取消股比限制,使特斯拉可选择独资而非合资。
2
Packages beat single tools

Equity, land, tax and finance together made the plant fast to build.

一揽子优于单一工具
股比、土地、税收与金融共同加快了建厂速度。
3
FDI policy is a bargain

The host gains jobs and exports, the firm gains access, with commitments on both sides.

外资政策是一场交易
东道国获得就业与出口,企业获得市场准入,双方都要履行承诺。
